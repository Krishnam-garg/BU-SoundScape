Where we stand
versus the WHO

The WHO’s Guidelines for Community Noise recommend staying under 55 dB outdoors in the daytime (to avoid serious annoyance) and 45 dB at night, with 50 dB marking moderate annoyance. Inside classrooms WHO targets about 35 dB. The dashed line shows the limit that applies to each slot.
